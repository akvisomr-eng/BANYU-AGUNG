## Missing evidence to collect
Major ions (Ca, Mg, Na, K, HCO3, Cl, SO4); silica, alkalinity, hardness and conductivity; relevant contaminants (As, Pb, Cd, Hg, fluoride); current microbiology report; source flow; seasonal sampling; source and process point coordinates; chain-of-custody and lab accreditation.

## Publication rule
No public page, packaging artwork, advertisement, product specification, or sales material may convert these secondary values into a current guarantee that packaged product is safe, compliant, mineral-rich, healthy, or certified. QA must upload and approve current applicable test reports and legal documents first. Keep each result linked to its original source, sample point, date, method, unit, detection limit and reviewer.
