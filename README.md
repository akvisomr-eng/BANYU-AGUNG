# BANYU AGUNG

Integrated public website and enterprise management system for the Jolotundo bottled-water business.

## Product goals
- Public brand, product, quality transparency, distributor and order information.
- Integrated ERP/management modules for procurement, production, quality assurance, inventory, sales, distribution, finance, HR, assets, compliance and reporting.
- Indonesian-first UX, role-based access, audit trails and tenant-ready architecture.

## Status
Foundation repository initialized. Detailed architecture, implementation plan and database migrations will be added on the feature branch.

## Critical quality note
Historical Jolotundo research data is contextual secondary evidence, not a current drinking-water certificate. No potable-water, mineral, health or regulatory compliance claim may be published without current accredited laboratory reports and applicable permits.
